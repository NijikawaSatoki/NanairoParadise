Abvalese (k̈̑′ĭ̥̃ṇḍ̃)

About

Abvalese (k̈̑′ĭ̥̃ṇḍ̃ /c͡ʎʼɯ̽̃ɳᶯɖ˨˩/) is the language spoken by the ab'vali, a species of avian-esque people living in the Isolated Isles, close to the Uokra ice cap in the south. Not much is known about the language, except for what those banished from the islands were willing to disclose.
