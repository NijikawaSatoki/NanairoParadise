UIDUL Transcription for the Abvalese Language

About the alphabet

The Abvalese language has a writing system similar to cuneiform, however it represents concepts as opposed to spoken sounds. As such, the Universal Institute for the Development and Understanding of the World's Languages (UIDUL) developed a transcription alphabet based on how those that got banished from the Isolated Isles describe the phonetic system used for Abvalese. That transcription is what'll be used throughout this page.

Vowels

All vowels also have contrastive nasalization before nasal consonants. In other words, before the consonants m, m̈, m̀, m̈̀, n, ṇ, n̈, ǹ, n̋. Vowels can be long or short.

Tone marks

Abvalese has 20 known tones that a syllable can have. They are high flat, mid-high flat, mid flat, mid-low flat, low flat, high falling, mid-high falling, mid falling, mid-low falling, mid-high rising, mid rising, mid-low rising, low rising, high dipping, mid peaking, mid dipping, low peaking, high variable, low variable, and true variable.

The high flat tone is written with a double line above (◌̿). For example: a̿, ã̿, ä̿, ä̃̿, ă̿, ẵ̿, æ̿, æ̃̿, e̿, ẽ̿, ë̿, ë̃̿, ə̿, ə̃̿, i̿, ĩ̿, ĭ̿, ĭ̃̿, ï̿, ï̃̿, ĭ̈̿, ĭ̈̃̿, o̿, õ̿, ö̿, ö̃̿, ŏ̿, ŏ̃̿, œ̿, œ̃̿, u̿, ũ̿, ŭ̿, ŭ̃̿, ü̿, ü̃̿, ŭ̈̿, ŭ̈̃̿, y̆̿, y̆̃̿, ẏ̿, ỹ̇̿, ÿ̿, ÿ̃̿.

The mid-high flat tone is written with a line above (◌̅). For example: a̅, ã̅, ä̅, ä̃̅, ă̅, ẵ̅, æ̅, æ̃̅, e̅, ẽ̅, ë̅, ë̃̅, ə̅, ə̃̅, i̅, ĩ̅, ĭ̅, ĭ̃̅, ï̅, ï̃̅, ĭ̈̅, ĭ̈̃̅, o̅, õ̅, ö̅, ö̃̅, ŏ̅, ŏ̃̅, œ̅, œ̃̅, u̅, ũ̅, ŭ̅, ŭ̃̅, ü̅, ü̃̅, ŭ̈̅, ŭ̈̃̅, y̆̅, y̆̃̅, ẏ̅, ỹ̇̅, ÿ̅, ÿ̃̅.

The mid-high rising tone is written with a hook above (◌̉). For example: ả, ã̉, ä̉, ä̃̉, ẳ, ẵ̉, æ̉, æ̃̉, ẻ, ẽ̉, ë̉, ë̃̉, ə̉, ə̃̉, ỉ, ĩ̉, ĭ̉, ĭ̃̉, ï̉, ï̃̉, ĭ̈̉, ĭ̈̃̉, ỏ, õ̉, ö̉, ö̃̉, ŏ̉, ŏ̃̉, œ̉, œ̃̉, ủ, ũ̉, ŭ̉, ŭ̃̉, ü̉, ü̃̉, ŭ̈̉, ŭ̈̃̉, y̆̉, y̆̃̉, ẏ̉, ỹ̇̉, ÿ̉, ÿ̃̉.

The mid rising tone is written with an acute accent (◌́). For example: á, ã́, ä́, ä̃́, ắ, ẵ́, ǽ, æ̃́, é, ẽ́, ë́, ë̃́, ə́, ə̃́, í, ĩ́, ĭ́, ĭ̃́, ḯ, ï̃́, ĭ̈́, ĭ̈̃́, ó, ṍ, ö́, ö̃́, ŏ́, ŏ̃́, œ́, œ̃́, ú, ṹ, ŭ́, ŭ̃́, ǘ, ü̃́, ŭ̈́, ŭ̈̃́, y̆́, y̆̃́, ẏ́, ỹ̇́, ÿ́, ÿ̃́.

The mid-low rising tone is written with an inverted breve below (◌̯). For example: a̯, ã̯, ä̯, ä̯̃, ă̯, ẵ̯, æ̯, æ̯̃, e̯, ẽ̯, ë̯, ë̯̃, ə̯, ə̯̃, i̯, ĩ̯, ĭ̯, ĭ̯̃, ï̯, ï̯̃, ĭ̯̈, ĭ̯̈̃, o̯, õ̯, ö̯, ö̯̃, ŏ̯, ŏ̯̃, œ̯, œ̯̃, u̯, ũ̯, ŭ̯, ŭ̯̃, ü̯, ü̯̃, ŭ̯̈, ŭ̯̈̃, y̯̆, y̯̆̃, ẏ̯, ỹ̯̇, ÿ̯, ÿ̯̃.

The low rising tone is written with a double acute accent (◌̋). For example: a̋, ã̋, ä̋, ä̃̋, ă̋, ẵ̋, æ̋, æ̃̋, e̋, ẽ̋, ë̋, ë̃̋, ə̋, ə̃̋, i̋, ĩ̋, ĭ̋, ĭ̃̋, ï̋, ï̃̋, ĭ̈̋, ĭ̈̃̋, ő, õ̋, ö̋, ö̃̋, ŏ̋, ŏ̃̋, œ̋, œ̃̋, ű, ũ̋, ŭ̋, ŭ̃̋, ü̋, ü̃̋, ŭ̈̋, ŭ̈̃̋, y̆̋, y̆̃̋, ẏ̋, ỹ̇̋, ÿ̋, ÿ̃̋.

The high dipping tone is written with a caron (◌̌). For example: ǎ, ã̌, ä̌, ä̃̌, ă̌, ẵ̌, æ̌, æ̃̌, ě, ẽ̌, ë̌, ë̃̌, ə̌, ə̃̌, ǐ, ĩ̌, ĭ̌, ĭ̃̌, ï̌, ï̃̌, ĭ̈̌, ĭ̈̃̌, ǒ, õ̌, ö̌, ö̃̌, ŏ̌, ŏ̃̌, œ̌, œ̃̌, ǔ, ũ̌, ŭ̌, ŭ̃̌, ǚ, ü̃̌, ŭ̈̌, ŭ̈̃̌, y̆̌, y̆̃̌, ẏ̌, ỹ̇̌, ÿ̌, ÿ̃̌.

The mid peaking tone is written with a circumflex (◌̂). For example: â, ã̂, ä̂, ä̃̂, ă̂, ẵ̂, æ̂, æ̃̂, ê, ẽ̂, ë̂, ë̃̂, ə̂, ə̃̂, î, ĩ̂, ĭ̂, ĭ̃̂, ï̂, ï̃̂, ĭ̈̂, ĭ̈̃̂, ô, õ̂, ö̂, ö̃̂, ŏ̂, ŏ̃̂, œ̂, œ̃̂, û, ũ̂, ŭ̂, ŭ̃̂, ü̂, ü̃̂, ŭ̈̂, ŭ̈̃̂, y̆̂, y̆̃̂, ẏ̂, ỹ̇̂, ÿ̂, ÿ̃̂.

The mid dipping tone is written with a caron below (◌̬). For example: a̬, ã̬, ä̬, ä̬̃, ă̬, ẵ̬, æ̬, æ̬̃, e̬, ẽ̬, ë̬, ë̬̃, ə̬, ə̬̃, i̬, ĩ̬, ĭ̬, ĭ̬̃, ï̬, ï̬̃, ĭ̬̈, ĭ̬̈̃, o̬, õ̬, ö̬, ö̬̃, ŏ̬, ŏ̬̃, œ̬, œ̬̃, u̬, ũ̬, ŭ̬, ŭ̬̃, ü̬, ü̬̃, ŭ̬̈, ŭ̬̈̃, y̬̆, y̬̆̃, ẏ̬, ỹ̬̇, ÿ̬, ÿ̬̃.

The low peaking tone is written with a circumflex below (◌̭). For example: a̭, ã̭, ä̭, ä̭̃, ă̭, ẵ̭, æ̭, æ̭̃, ḙ, ḙ̃, ḙ̈, ḙ̈̃, ə̭, ə̭̃, i̭, ĩ̭, ĭ̭, ĭ̭̃, ï̭, ï̭̃, ĭ̭̈, ĭ̭̈̃, o̭, õ̭, ö̭, ö̭̃, ŏ̭, ŏ̭̃, œ̭, œ̭̃, ṷ, ṷ̃, ṷ̆, ṷ̆̃, ṷ̈, ṷ̈̃, ṷ̆̈, ṷ̆̈̃, y̭̆, y̭̆̃, ẏ̭, ỹ̭̇, ÿ̭, ÿ̭̃.

The high variable tone is written with a double vertical line above (◌̎). For example: a̎, ã̎, ä̎, ä̃̎, ă̎, ẵ̎, æ̎, æ̃̎, e̎, ẽ̎, ë̎, ë̃̎, ə̎, ə̃̎, i̎, ĩ̎, ĭ̎, ĭ̃̎, ï̎, ï̃̎, ĭ̈̎, ĭ̈̃̎, o̎, õ̎, ö̎, ö̃̎, ŏ̎, ŏ̃̎, œ̎, œ̃̎, u̎, ũ̎, ŭ̎, ŭ̃̎, ü̎, ü̃̎, ŭ̈̎, ŭ̈̃̎, y̆̎, y̆̃̎, ẏ̎, ỹ̇̎, ÿ̎, ÿ̃̎.

A note...

As you might notice with some of the tone examples, the diacritics might not line up so nicely. This is sadly entirely an issue on the font front, due to fonts having wildly varying support for the combining diacritics and diacritic stacking. If you've ever tried having a font display what is often referred to as “Zalgo text”, then you might know what I'm talking about.
